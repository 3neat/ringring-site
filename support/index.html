Help with the game

Contact

Write to support@neatneatneat.dev. Tell us what you were doing, your phone model, and the app version (shown in Profile).

Common questions

A friend's invite link opens Safari instead of the game. Make sure Ring Ring is installed, then press and hold the link and choose "Open in Ring Ring". You can also type the six-character code under Join a match.

My turn timer ran out. The host chooses how long the table waits for each player. After that the game plays a safe turn for you: a pass, or your lowest card if you were leading.

A ruling looks wrong. After a hand, tap Report ruling and describe what happened. Please leave out card faces and the deal.

I want to remove my data. Delete hand history in the app, or delete the app. Matches on our server are deleted automatically a day after they end. See the Privacy Policy.
