Privacy Policy

Ring Ring · Effective October 4, 2026

Ring Ring is a card game for two to four players, each on their own device. This page explains what the game and its server do with information, in plain words.

The short version

  • There is no account. You pick a display name and play.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics, and we do not sell or rent information.
  • We do not use your contacts, location, photos, microphone or advertising identifier.
  • Your hand history stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your display name, settings, and your hand history (a replayable record of each completed hand) are stored only on your device. Hand history is kept for 30 days and you can delete any record, or all of them, from the app.

Who sees your information

  • Other players in your match see your display name and the plays you make, as in any card game. They never see your cards.
  • Service providers. The game runs on Google Cloud, and Apple delivers notifications and the app. They process information to provide those services and not for their own advertising of Ring Ring.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Inviting friends

An invitation is a link or a QR code. If you invite someone from your contacts, the app only opens the Messages composer with the link. We do not read, upload or store your contacts.

Your choices

  • Choose any display name. It does not have to be your real name.
  • Turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Delete hand history in the app. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.
  • Match data on our server is deleted automatically as described above. To ask for earlier deletion, write to support@neatneatneat.dev.

Children

Ring Ring is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Beta

Ring Ring is in beta. If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
